    - Charged off past 36 Months? (DateTimeDiff Report_Date / Charge Off Date < 36 months)
    - Average Annual Loss Rate (static 0 when no loss rate data)
    - OutputFilePath_Dropped (string construction with hardcoded UNC prefix)

Stage 5 | Static Pool / Vintage Cohort Construction
  XML label: "Get the number of years to include for static pool and years until charge off"
  Key tools: Join (to prior period client file), Formula tools
  Key fields produced:
    - Year 0 through Year 6 (boolean day-range flags from Days from Origination)
    - Expected Loss - Year 1 through Year 7 (joined from prior period: [Right_Expected Loss - Year N])
